# Helping a Customer Locate a Movie

**Role:** Customer Support Representative  
**Area:** Customer Experience · Content Discovery · Self-Service

## Situation

A customer contacted support because she could not find a particular movie on the streaming platform and was unhappy with the experience.

## What I did

I apologised for the inconvenience, asked for the movie title and searched the platform.

I located the movie and provided the customer with a direct link.

Rather than ending the interaction there, I also walked her through how to find the content herself in the future.

## Outcome

The customer was able to access the movie and was satisfied with the support provided.

## What this demonstrates

- Customer empathy
- Content/platform navigation
- Fast problem solving
- Customer education
- Encouraging self-service
