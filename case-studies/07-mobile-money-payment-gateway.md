# Introducing Mobile Money Payments for Ghanaian Users

**Role:** IT Manager  
**Area:** Payment Operations · Vendor Management · Revenue Enablement

## Situation

As the platform expanded in Ghana and other African markets, customer feedback indicated that some Ghanaian users preferred mobile-money payments.

The existing payment options did not adequately serve that preference.

## What I did

I researched payment-gateway options and identified **Tingg Africa** as a potential provider.

I contacted the company, established a contact person and explained the platform's requirements.

The provider supplied onboarding documentation. I forwarded the documentation to the CEO, and it went through the company's legal review process.

After approval, the service was onboarded and integrated into the platform.

I also worked with other payment-gateway providers including Paystack and Flutterwave in relation to platform operations and integrations.

## Reported outcome

According to the work information available to me, the introduction of mobile-money payments contributed to a reported **7% increase in company revenue**, with users who previously could not pay through the available methods becoming able to subscribe.
