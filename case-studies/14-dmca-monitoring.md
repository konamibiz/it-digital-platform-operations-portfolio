# DMCA Content Monitoring and Takedown Operations

**Role:** IT & Digital/Platform Operations  
**Area:** Content Protection · Digital Operations

## Situation

The company produced and owned digital content that was being redistributed on other online platforms.

## What I did

I monitored online platforms for unauthorized use and redistribution of the company's content. My responsibilities included:

Conducting regular searches for company-owned films, videos and other digital assets across online platforms.
Identifying potentially infringing uploads, pages, channels and websites.
Documenting relevant URLs and infringement details for reporting.
Initiating copyright/DMCA takedown requests through the appropriate platform processes.
Tracking reported cases and following up where necessary.
Escalating cases that required additional review or supporting documentation.
Maintaining awareness of platform-specific copyright reporting requirements and procedures.

## Business purpose

The work supported the company's efforts to protect its digital catalogue, limit unauthorized distribution and preserve legitimate audience traffic and monetization opportunities across its official platforms.

It also provided a structured process for identifying and responding to unauthorized use of company-owned content online.
