# Launching a Referral Program for Subscriber Growth

**Role:** IT Manager  
**Area:** Product Initiative · Subscriber Growth · Vendor Coordination

## Situation

The company wanted to increase active paid subscribers and create additional ways for existing users to bring new customers to the platform.

## Recommendation

I proposed introducing a referral bonus programme to the CEO.

The proposal was approved for implementation.

## Implementation

I liaised with the outsourced application-development team to discuss how the feature should work.

The proposed flow included a referral-code field during registration. When an existing customer referred a new paid customer, the referring account would receive additional subscription time as the referral reward.

## Reported outcome

After implementation, more users began referring new customers to the platform.

The work notes record a **2% increase in active paid subscribers** following the initiative.

## What this demonstrates

- Growth-oriented product thinking
- Feature specification
- Technology/vendor coordination
- Subscriber acquisition strategy
- Measuring business outcomes
