# Career Journey

## From Customer Support to IT & Digital/Platform Operations

My career progression has been shaped by working at the intersection of **customers, technology, platforms and business operations**.

I started close to the customer, moved into team leadership, and later took responsibility for coordinating technology and platform operations. My responsibilities subsequently expanded into digital-platform operations, audience growth, analytics and content operations.

---

## 1. Customer Support Representative

At the customer-support level, my focus was understanding the customer's problem, investigating the issue and helping the customer reach a practical resolution.

### Examples of work

- Helped customers locate content on the streaming platform.
- Investigated account and password-reset issues.
- Assisted customers with subscription activation after bank-transfer payments.
- Explained processes to customers so they could resolve similar issues themselves.

This stage gave me a strong understanding of how customers actually interact with a digital platform.

---

## 2. Customer Support Team Lead

As Team Lead, my responsibilities expanded from resolving individual customer issues to handling escalations, identifying recurring problems and helping the wider support team work more effectively.

### Examples of work

- Investigated complex subscription-access issues.
- Troubleshot browser-specific video playback problems.
- Managed escalated payment and subscription cases.
- Created and maintained internal knowledge-base documentation.
- Identified recurring customer problems and routed them to the appropriate teams.
- Organised training and support for customer-service representatives.
- Contributed ideas for subscriber growth, including referral and campus-ambassador initiatives.

This stage strengthened my troubleshooting, escalation management, communication and process-improvement skills.

---

## 3. IT Manager

As IT Manager, my responsibilities moved further into technology operations and coordination.

I worked with external technology providers and internal teams on areas including:

- website and mobile application changes;
- hosting and CDN operations;
- application testing;
- payment-gateway integration;
- platform resource planning;
- technology feature recommendations;
- server-capacity planning;
- technology projects; and
- management recommendations.

A key part of the role was acting as a bridge between management, users/support teams and external technology providers.

I did not position myself as the developer of the applications or the technician repairing third-party hosted servers. My responsibility was to communicate requirements, coordinate vendors and technical teams, oversee testing, escalate issues, monitor resolution and report to management.

---

## 4. IT & Digital/Platform Operations

My responsibilities also expanded into digital-platform operations.

This included:

- YouTube channel operations;
- Facebook and Instagram operations;
- platform analytics;
- YouTube SEO;
- social-media monetisation;
- Facebook page verification;
- Google AdSense account coordination;
- content monitoring and DMCA takedown reporting;
- content publishing;
- audience-growth recommendations; and
- management reporting based on platform analytics.

This combination of IT, platform, customer and digital experience is the direction I am continuing to build professionally.
