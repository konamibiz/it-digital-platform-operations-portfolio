# DMCA Content Monitoring and Takedown Operations

**Role:** IT & Digital/Platform Operations  
**Area:** Content Protection · Digital Operations

## Situation

The company produced and owned digital content that was being redistributed on other online platforms.

## What I did

I carried out regular searches for company content across online platforms.

When I identified potentially infringing links, pages or channels, I reported them for takedown through the appropriate copyright/DMCA process.

## Business purpose

The objective was to reduce unauthorised redistribution and help direct audiences toward the company's official channels and platforms.

## What this demonstrates

- Digital content monitoring
- Copyright/DMCA workflow awareness
- Online research
- Content-protection operations
- Digital revenue protection
