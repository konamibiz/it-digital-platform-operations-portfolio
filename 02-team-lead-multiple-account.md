# Resolving a Multiple-Account Subscription Access Issue

**Role:** Customer Support Team Lead  
**Area:** Escalation Management · Account Troubleshooting

## Situation

An escalated customer could not watch videos even though the support representative had confirmed that an active subscription existed on the account.

## Investigation

I independently checked the account and confirmed that the subscription was active.

During the call, I asked whether the customer had another account. She initially said no, but confirmed that she had another email address.

Instead of pressing for the email immediately, I guided the customer through checking the email address currently being used to log in.

We discovered that she was logged into a different account that did not have the active subscription. The subscription was attached to another account.

## Resolution

I guided the customer through logging out and signing in with the email address associated with the active subscription.

We then tested video playback together and confirmed that the customer could watch the videos.

## Outcome

The customer regained access and was satisfied with the resolution.

## What this demonstrates

- Escalation management
- Root-cause investigation
- Account-level troubleshooting
- Customer communication
- Guided troubleshooting
- Avoiding assumptions during investigation
