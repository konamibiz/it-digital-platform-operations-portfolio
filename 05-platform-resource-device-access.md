# Managing Platform Resource Usage Through Device Access Controls

**Role:** IT Manager  
**Area:** Platform Operations · Product Requirements · Technology Coordination

## Situation

The platform was seeing high video usage around the release of a popular weekly series. Video-view activity appeared significantly higher than the number of paid users.

I identified account sharing as a likely contributor to excessive platform usage.

The infrastructure had originally been planned around approximately 50,000–70,000 active-user usage per month, while observed usage was reported at more than 100,000–200,000 usage levels.

This created pressure on server resources and increased the risk of buffering, downtime and additional infrastructure costs.

## Recommendation

During a meeting with the CEO, I proposed introducing a **multiple-device login** feature to help control account usage.

The CEO approved the proposal.

## Implementation

I worked with the technology/development team to explain the expected behaviour of the feature.

After implementation, the feature was tested rigorously before being launched on both the mobile application and website.

## Reported outcome

Following implementation, the company observed an increase in paid users. The figures recorded in my work notes were approximately:

- 10% increase after the initial launch;
- approximately 20% by the end of the first month.

The work also showed an improvement in controlling platform resource usage.

## My contribution

My role was not to develop or repair the servers. I:

- identified the operational/business problem;
- proposed a product solution;
- communicated the requirement;
- coordinated with the technical team;
- participated in testing; and
- monitored the outcome.
