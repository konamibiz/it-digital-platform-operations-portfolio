# Building a Support Knowledge Base

**Role:** Customer Support Team Lead  
**Area:** Support Operations · Documentation · Self-Service

## Situation

The support team handled recurring questions and technical issues that could often be resolved using a consistent procedure.

## What I did

I created internal knowledge-base documentation that served as a quick reference for support representatives.

I also contributed to public-facing support documentation covering common issues such as:

- video buffering;
- payment questions;
- subscription questions; and
- video-download questions.

I also identified recurring problems reported by support representatives and routed technical issues to the technical team and billing/financial issues to the appropriate department.

## Outcome

The documentation gave representatives a consistent reference for handling recurring issues and helped customers resolve some problems without contacting support.

## What this demonstrates

- Knowledge management
- Process documentation
- Support operations
- Recurring-problem analysis
- Cross-functional escalation
