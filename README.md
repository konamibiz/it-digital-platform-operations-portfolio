## About Me

I am an IT, Digital Operations and Platform Management professional with professional experience spanning August 2016 to the present.

My career has developed from customer support into team leadership, IT management and broader digital and platform operations. This progression has given me practical experience across customer-facing services, technical coordination, platform management and operational improvement.
