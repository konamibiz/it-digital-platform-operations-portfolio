## About Me

I'm an IT, Digital Operations and Platform Management professional with a career that has grown from customer support into team leadership, IT management and broader digital-platform operations.

My experience comes from working closely with customers, internal teams, management and external technology providers to keep digital platforms running, resolve operational challenges and improve how services are delivered.

Over the years, I've been involved in website and mobile application operations, hosting and CDN coordination, payment integrations, application testing, technology vendor management, YouTube and social-media operations, digital analytics, SEO and content protection.

One thing my experience has taught me is that technology operations go beyond keeping systems running. They also involve understanding the people who use those systems, identifying what isn't working and coordinating the right people to get things moving.

This portfolio showcases selected projects, challenges I've worked on, operational decisions and lessons from my professional journey.
