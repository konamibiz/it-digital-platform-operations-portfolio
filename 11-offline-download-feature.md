# Introducing Offline Video Downloads

**Role:** IT Manager  
**Area:** Product Improvement · Customer Retention · Platform Operations

## Situation

As the customer base grew, recurring support feedback showed that users were concerned about data consumption and network reliability when streaming videos.

The issue was particularly relevant for customers in Nigeria and other African markets.

## Recommendation

I proposed adding an offline-download feature to the Android and iOS applications.

The objective was to allow subscribers to download videos using an internet connection and watch them later without continuous data usage.

I presented the business case to the CEO, including the potential customer-retention and platform-resource benefits.

## Implementation

The feature was approved and implemented.

The company then communicated the new functionality to customers.

## Observed effects

The company observed that some previous customers who had not renewed for some time began renewing after the feature was introduced.

The download model also reduced the number of people simultaneously streaming some content, helping manage server bandwidth.
