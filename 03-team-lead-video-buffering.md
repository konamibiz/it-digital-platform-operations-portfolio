# Troubleshooting Browser-Specific Video Playback

**Role:** Customer Support Team Lead  
**Area:** Technical Support · Remote Troubleshooting · Knowledge Management

## Situation

A customer with an active subscription reported that videos continuously buffered on a Mac computer while playback worked on a mobile device.

The issue had already been escalated after the assigned support representative could not resolve it.

## Investigation

I contacted the customer and gathered information about:

- when the problem started;
- the device being used;
- whether playback worked elsewhere; and
- the browser being used.

The customer was using Safari. Other platform functions were working, but the platform videos were not playing correctly.

## Resolution

I suggested testing the platform with another browser to determine whether the problem was browser-specific.

I guided the customer through installing Chrome, logging into the platform and testing playback.

The videos played successfully in Chrome.

## Follow-up

I documented the resolution in the knowledge base so that support representatives could use the same troubleshooting approach for future cases.

## What this demonstrates

- Structured troubleshooting
- Browser/device investigation
- Remote customer support
- Escalation handling
- Knowledge management

> Note: The portfolio describes this as a browser-specific playback issue rather than claiming a definitive technical root cause that was not established during the support interaction.
